## How BeWild’s Gemini activation process works

The published Gemini guide describes a three-stage process:

1. Choose a Gemini plan.
2. Log in to your own Google account and submit the required browser information.
3. Pay and wait for the subscription to be activated.

BeWild says the full process generally takes around three to five minutes before payment, while activation may take approximately five to thirty minutes after payment.

The important technical detail is that BeWild’s Gemini workflow asks for Google account cookies exported from `myaccount.google.com`. The service’s guide recommends using the Cookie-Editor browser extension and pasting the exported JSON into the BeWild form.

That information is highly sensitive. A Google session cookie can function like an active login credential. Even though BeWild’s own instructions describe it as part of the activation process, users should treat the file as private account-access data:

- Do not send the cookie JSON to a person through chat or email.
- Do not paste it into an unrelated website.
- Use the same browser session only when you understand the workflow.
- Remove or invalidate the session after the subscription process if appropriate.
- Avoid using an account containing sensitive business, financial, or recovery information unless you accept the account-security implications.

BeWild’s documentation itself tells users to keep the cookie private and says it is intended only for the activation process.

This is one of the biggest differences between a direct Google purchase and a third-party activation service. With Google, payment and account management happen inside Google’s own subscription system. With BeWild, the process may involve handing a temporary authenticated browser credential to a service so it can complete the upgrade.

## Which Gemini tier makes sense?

The right choice depends more on how often you hit usage limits than on the word “Pro.”

### Gemini Plus

Plus is the lowest paid option listed in BeWild’s Gemini guide. It may be suitable if the free version is too restrictive but you do not regularly use long documents, intensive research, or demanding creative features.

Choose Plus when:

- You use Gemini occasionally throughout the week.
- You want a paid tier without jumping to Pro-level pricing.
- Your prompts are mostly short questions, drafting, or light planning.
- You are testing whether a paid plan fits your routine.

The exact limits and included Google benefits should be checked on the live product page. BeWild’s public tutorial confirms the plan name but does not publish a full feature matrix.

### Gemini Pro

Pro is the more sensible middle choice for regular users. Google’s official AI Pro plan is designed around higher Gemini limits, access to Pro models, Deep Research, and 5 TB of storage.

Choose Pro when:

- You use Gemini for work or study several times per week.
- You regularly upload files or work with longer context.
- You want expanded access without paying for an Ultra tier.
- You want Google storage and AI features in the same plan.
- You are looking for the standard premium Gemini experience rather than maximum usage.

For many people, Pro is where the benefits become easier to justify. Ultra can be useful, but the price difference is large enough that occasional users may pay for capacity they never use.

### Gemini Ultra 5x

Ultra 5x is aimed at heavier users. The “5x” label indicates a higher usage level relative to the relevant Pro baseline, but it should not be treated as a guarantee that every feature works exactly five times as often. Usage limits can vary by model, tool, context length, and system demand.

Choose Ultra 5x when:

- You work with Gemini for long sessions.
- You regularly reach Pro limits.
- You create or analyze large amounts of content.
- You use several Google AI tools rather than only chat.
- The lower Ultra tier gives you enough capacity without the maximum price.

### Gemini Ultra 20x

Ultra 20x is for users who can genuinely consume a large amount of AI capacity. It is difficult to justify for light chat, occasional writing, or basic summarization.

Choose Ultra 20x only when:

- You have already tested a lower plan and repeatedly hit limits.
- You use Gemini as part of a high-volume workflow.
- The additional capacity directly saves time or supports paid work.

A higher multiplier does not automatically mean better answers for every prompt. It generally relates to available usage capacity, not a promise that every response will be more accurate or creative.
